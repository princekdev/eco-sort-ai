# EcoSort AI — Project Description

## Project Title

**EcoSort AI: AI-Powered Waste Segregation & Sustainability Assistant**

**Tagline:** _"Understand Waste. Sort Smarter. Build a Sustainable Future."_

---

## Problem

People often do not know:

- Which waste category an item belongs to
- Whether an item is recyclable
- Whether it should be reused or composted
- How different types of waste should be disposed of
- Why correct waste segregation matters

This leads to:

- Incorrect waste segregation
- Reduced recycling efficiency
- Increased landfill waste
- Environmental pollution
- Loss of reusable materials
- Methane emissions from organic waste in landfills
- Unsafe handling of hazardous materials

---

## Target Users

**Primary users:**
- Students (secondary school, college, and university level)
- Households making daily waste decisions
- School and college sustainability committees

**Secondary users:**
- Office sustainability coordinators
- Community leaders and NGO workers
- Waste management awareness educators

---

## SDG Alignment

### Primary SDG

**SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production**

EcoSort AI directly supports SDG 12 by:
- Educating users on responsible waste disposal
- Promoting recycling awareness for common materials
- Encouraging reuse before disposal
- Highlighting responsible consumption habits
- Providing guidance on hazardous waste handling

### Secondary SDGs

**SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities and Communities**
- Better waste systems contribute to cleaner, more livable communities
- Accessible waste guidance supports urban sustainability goals

**SDG 13 – Climate Action**
- Composting organic waste reduces methane emissions (a potent greenhouse gas)
- Recycling reduces energy consumption vs. primary material production
- Hazardous waste guidance prevents soil and water contamination

---

## Problem Statement

> **"How might we use AI to help people correctly identify, segregate, and responsibly manage everyday waste so that communities can become more sustainable?"**

---

## AI Solution

EcoSort AI uses AI to provide:

1. **Waste Classification** — Natural language input classified into one of 8 waste categories
2. **Decision Support** — Recommended disposal action (Recycle, Compost, Reuse, Special Disposal, General Waste)
3. **Conversational Assistant** — Open question-answer interface for sustainability guidance
4. **Sustainability Education** — Context, tips, and SDG connections for every classification
5. **Responsible AI Design** — Transparency, uncertainty acknowledgment, and no unsafe guidance

## Expected Impact

### Environmental Impact (Potential)
- Improved waste segregation accuracy

- Greater public understanding of hazardous waste handling

### Social Impact (Potential)
- Accessible sustainability guidance for students and households

- Empowerment of individuals to take practical sustainability action

- Recovery potential of recyclable materials currently lost to landfill
- Reduced waste management costs through better source separation
- Encouragement of reuse, reducing replacement purchase costs

> **Note:** All impact metrics are illustrative targets for a deployed version of EcoSort AI. This is a prototype developed as part of the 1M1B AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React (JavaScript)
- **Routing:** React Router DOM
- **Styling:** Custom CSS (no external framework required)
- **AI Logic:** Local knowledge base (no API keys or cloud services required)
- **Development tool:** IBM BOB
- **Deployment:** Runs locally — `npm start`
